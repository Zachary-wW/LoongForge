About LoongForge

A training framework born from real-world, large-scale production workloads — and shared back with the community.

🐉 The Baige "Loong" Family

LoongForge is part of the Loong open-source series from Baidu's Baige AI infrastructure platform — named after the traditional Chinese loong boat, a symbol of coordinated power and direction.

Before becoming open source, LoongForge had already empowered numerous enterprise use cases with its robust training acceleration and scaling capabilities:

  • Powers proprietary large-scale models across Education, Computer Vision, and Embodied AI.
  • Typically achieves a 30%~50% speedup over standard customer baselines.
  • Supports ultra-large cluster training scaling to 5,000+ XPUs.

Sister projects include LoongFlow for AI workflow orchestration.

🚀 Roadmap

📦 Expanded foundation models

Kimi 2.5 / 2.6, DeepSeek-V4 and next-generation open weights.

🤖 Embodied AI

DreamZero and LingBot VA.

🎬 Diffusion acceleration

Continued performance work on the WAN family.

🧩 Heterogeneous DP

Full heterogeneous DP memory optimization + parallelism compatibility.

⚖️ Advanced MoE load balancing

New strategies for skewed expert routing at scale.

🔢 INT4 QAT

Quantization-aware training, inspired by Kimi 2.5.

📜 Long-sequence training

ChunkPipe scheduling and Context Parallelism (CP).

🎯 MTP scaling

Real-world Multi-Token Prediction for better speculative decoding.
